GSK Tour & Travels — the logo

The idea: the G is a steering wheel — a ring, a spoke running into an orange hub — and the orange arrowhead at its open end is the direction of travel. S and K are drawn in the same heavy monoline, so the wordmark reads as one engineered piece rather than typed letters. It needs no photo, gradient or effect, which is why it embroiders, prints and stamps cleanly.

Usage rules

Clear space
Keep free space equal to twice the thickness of the G ring on all sides.
Minimum size
Full lockup: 28 mm / 110 px wide. Horizontal: 24 mm / 90 px. Below that use the emblem or favicon.
On green & dark
Always use the reversed (paper + orange) files. Never place the ink version on the green shirt.
Don’t
Stretch, rotate, recolour the letters, add shadows or outlines, or place on busy photos without a dark overlay.
